WikiPoly Bar

One-click language switching on official Wikipedia, plus a same-tab split view for comparing editions.

Preferred languages

Order matters. The first language is Alt+1 / Alt+Shift+1.

Behavior

Drag the dotted handle to move the bar. The stacked window buttons minimize or close it. After close, click the small W chip (or enable “Show floating bar”) to bring it back.

Shortcuts

  • Alt+1 … 9 — jump to that preferred language when Chrome does not steal the key.
  • Alt+Shift+1 … 3 — same action, registered as extension commands.
  • Alt+Shift+S — open or close split view.
  • Esc — close split view.

Remap commands in Chrome at chrome://extensions/shortcuts

Privacy

WikiPoly Bar never sends your reading history anywhere. Language preferences stay in Chrome sync storage. Split view only frames official *.wikipedia.org pages. Unlike Wikiwand, you stay on Wikipedia and keep your account session on that edition.
