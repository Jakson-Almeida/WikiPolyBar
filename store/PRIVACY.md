# Privacy policy — WikiPoly Bar

Last updated: 17 August 2026

WikiPoly Bar is a Chrome extension that adds a language switcher and split view to official Wikipedia articles (`*.wikipedia.org`).

## What we collect

The extension stores, in Chrome’s `storage.sync` (tied to your Google account if Chrome Sync is on):

- preferred Wikipedia languages and their order
- whether the bar is visible or minimized
- bar position on the screen
- whether keyboard shortcuts are enabled
- whether split-view scroll sync is enabled

It does not create an account, does not collect browsing history, and does not send article titles, URLs, or language choices to our servers.

The landing-page waitlist (if you use it) is the only optional email we might receive, and it is separate from the extension.

## How the extension uses Wikipedia

On Wikipedia article pages the extension:

- reads language links already on the page, or asks the Wikipedia API on that same site, so it can offer matching editions
- can open another language edition in the same tab (split view)

Split view may strip framing restrictions on Wikipedia **subframes only**, so two official pages can appear side by side. Top-level Wikipedia browsing is unchanged.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share extension storage data. Wikipedia itself receives the normal requests your browser makes when you open an article.
